Four seasons of curb appeal

A porch for every season

A peek at the palettes and plants we love to work with throughout the year.

Spring

Fresh, soft, and new: tulips, pansies, ranunculus, ferns, and trailing ivy.

Summer

Lush and overflowing: petunias, calibrachoa, mandevilla, sweet potato vine, and Boston ferns.

Fall

Warm and harvest-rich: mums, ornamental kale, heuchera, grasses, and heirloom pumpkins.

Winter

Cozy and evergreen: fresh boughs, dwarf spruce, winterberry, birch poles, and lanterns.
